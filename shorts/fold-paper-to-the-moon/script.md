## Production notes
- **Hook frame: the sheet lies on the ground, not on a table** (guion asked for a small table):
  the 14-fold rung measures the column from the ground beside the observer, so it has to stand
  on the ground to be honest.
- **Bars:** linear in fold count = log scale of distance from 0.1 mm (length ∝ doublings); tagged LOG SCALE.
- **Too-small things at true scale:** the Moon in the hook gets a ring (no label); the station is
  drawn at symbol size with a marker; the ribbon/row widths are drawn ≥ 8 / 30 px (only lengths are measured).
- **Tower:** a generic symmetric stepped needle, not the Burj Khalifa's Y-plan spiral.
- **The voice did not leave the "wry beat" before "toilet paper"** (0.08 s between "of" and "toilet"); the music dips there instead.
